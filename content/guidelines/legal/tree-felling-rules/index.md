This document contains Annex 1 to [Decision N 1236-A](../decision-1236a-on-felling-trees/) of the Mayor of Yerevan (April 26, 2021), which establishes the procedure for tree felling and the accounting of resulting timber in public green areas of Yerevan.

Please note that item 13 mandates that for every tree subject to felling, a public notification must be posted on the tree at least 15 days in advance.

## The Procedure

1. This procedure regulates the relations concerning tree felling in public areas of Yerevan, accounting of the resulting timber, filling out tree felling tickets, and storing the resulting timber.
2. Tree felling in public green areas within the administrative borders of Yerevan is carried out by the "Greening and Environmental Protection" Community Non-Commercial Organization (CNCO) or leaseholder organizations of public green areas (hereinafter: organization) based on permission issued by an authorized official.
3. Tree felling permits are based on the requirements established by legislation.
4. Establish that the organization conducts an inventory and accounting of existing trees subject to felling (removal) in public areas of Yerevan twice a year: from April 1 to April 20, and from September 1 to September 20.
5. Based on the inventory results, a plan for the implementation of tree felling (removal) work is drawn up (hereinafter: plan). A tree felling ticket, following the form established in Annex 2, is completed for each tree subject to felling included in the plan. A timeframe is established for the execution of the plan, after which the organization submits information to the Environmental Protection Department of the Yerevan Municipality Staff (hereinafter: department).
6. The tree felling ticket is filled out by the organization.
7. Tree felling tickets are accountable documents that are numbered and prepared in duplicate. Numbering of tree felling tickets is performed by the department. One approved and numbered copy is provided to the organization, and the other is kept in the department to monitor the accounting of felled trees and timber volume.
8. In the event of unexecuted tree fellings, the approved tree felling tickets are returned to the department and voided.
9. Photographs must be attached to the tree felling ticket, showing the specific sections that justify the tree being subject to felling or removal, as well as the overall view of the tree.
10. The plan and tree felling tickets are submitted for approval to the Head of the Department before May 1 and October 1, respectively.
11. Plans and tree felling tickets are reviewed by the Head of the Department within a 10-day period from the date of submission.
12. After the approval of the tree felling ticket, the organization organizes and carries out public notification regarding the felling or removal of the specified tree.
13. **Public notification is carried out by posting a notice sheet in the form established by Annex 3 on the tree subject to felling (removal) at least fifteen days in advance.**
14. In cases of trees fallen due to emergencies, or to prevent emergency incidents in highly dangerous situations, the removal of hazardous trees prone to falling is carried out without a pre-approved tree felling ticket. In such cases, the tree felling ticket is submitted and approved after the removal of the fallen, hazardous trees.
15. The volume of timber is calculated in accordance with Order N 80-N of the Minister of Agriculture of the Republic of Armenia dated May 6, 2013.
