## Understanding the data

1. H and W: the average height and width of a mature specimen, in metres. These are typical landscape dimensions rather than record sizes: a street pit or a dry courtyard keeps a tree smaller, while an irrigated park lets it exceed them. For climbers, H is the height reached on a support and W the spread along it.
2. Z: the coldest USDA hardiness zone in which the species is normally hardy outdoors. Yerevan is 7b, so 7 or lower can be expected to survive the winter, 8 is marginal, and 9 or warmer needs protection. An asterisk marks a species rated warmer than the city.
3. OPALS: the [Ogren Plant Allergy Scale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ogren_Plant_Allergy_Scale), from 1 (least allergenic) to 10 (most). A dash means no published score was found. Scores are compiled from [published OPALS ratings by Thomas Leo Ogren](https://archive.org/details/allergyfreegarde00thom/).
