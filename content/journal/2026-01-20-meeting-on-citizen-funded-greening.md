On January 20, Kanach Yerevan met with city officials to discuss our initiative for [citizen-funded tree planting][1]. We agreed to utilize existing communication channels to launch a pilot project designed to demonstrate public interest and prove that residents are willing to fund, plant, and maintain urban greenery.
