+++
title = "Response received on severe tree damage on Nairi Zaryan Street"
+++

On January 18, we documented severe damage to the urban canopy caused by trenching activities on Nairi Zaryan Street. Initial observations indicate that the excavation significantly compromised tree root systems and bark, in apparent violation of municipal tree protection regulations.

On February 3, we received a response which basically states that this happened on private property, so no problem.
Which seems to be wrong, and we will continue this communication.
