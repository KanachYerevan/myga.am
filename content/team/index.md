Behind Kanach Yerevan is a small team of around 6 founding members, who define strategic goals and roadmaps.
All members are volunteers.
Core team members prefer to stay anonymous for now.

If you want to be a part of the broader Kanach Yerevan community, we'd be glad to have you on board!
We have a lot of existing ideas, and we are always open to new ones.
We'd love to help you find your place in the movement.

Please [contact us](/connect/) if you are interested in contributing in any way.
