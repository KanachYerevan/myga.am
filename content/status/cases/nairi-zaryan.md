* Status: request sent (18.01.2026).

In January 2026, work was done to replace pavement near the [new appartment building][1] at the intersection of Nairi Zaryan street and Azatutyan avenue.
The work failed to follow regulations imposed by Resolutions of the Council of Elders [N 36-N][2], which regulates protection of green zones, and [405-N][3], which regulates tree protection during construction works.

Excavation trenching was performed at a distance ranging from 20 to 90 cm from the trunk.
This is a direct violation of the 3.0-meter root protection zone required by municipal norms.
Furthermore, the failure to install mandatory protective fencing (boxing) resulted in severe mechanical damage to the trunk of one tree (a deep cut unrelated to the authorized work).
This constitutes a violation of the prohibition on damaging green plantations in common use areas.
