If you notice any unusual activity regarding trees in Yerevan—such as broken or sick trees, suspicious pruning, or new trees being planted—please let us know! Simply send us a message, and we will handle the situation or guide you on the next steps.

You can report in two ways:

- **Telegram Chat**: join our community chat [Kanach Yerevan](https://t.me/make_yerevan_green_again) and share your observation.
- **Telegram Bot**: send a message to our bot [@kanach_yerevan_bot](https://t.me/kanach_yerevan_bot). The bot allows you to stay completely anonymous and will guide you step-by-step on what information is needed.

Thank you for being a voice for Yerevan's trees! Every report helps us monitor the city's green spaces and take timely action. Your attention today helps create a greener Yerevan tomorrow.
