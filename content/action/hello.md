You found a piece of our movement in the physical world! We are a group of volunteers, experts, and concerned residents dedicated to **independent urban monitoring** and the systemic protection of Yerevan's urban forest.

### What we do

We transform concern for our city into professional action. We provide the technical standards, data collection, and legal monitoring that help ensure our trees are treated as vital infrastructure rather than disposable assets.

### How you can help right now

Even a small action helps protect our shared ecosystem:

- **Be a Watchdog:** If you see a tree being harmed, illegal construction, or "barbaric" pruning, use our [reporting guides](/action/report/) to take action.
- **Map the Canopy:** Help us catalog every tree in Yerevan. Use our [interactive map](/action/mapping/) to add trees in your neighborhood.
- **Stay Informed:** Read our [guidelines](/guidelines/) to understand why things like [leaving the leaves](/guidelines/leaves/) on the ground is vital for tree health.

### Join the Movement

We are always looking for "brains and hands" — researchers, photographers, lawyers, and observers.

- **Join our Telegram:** This is where we coordinate and share urgent updates. [Join the Chat](https://t.me/make_yerevan_green_again).
- **Become a Volunteer:** Check out our [active roles](/action/join/) and see where your skills fit best.

Let's make Yerevan green again, one tree at a time.
